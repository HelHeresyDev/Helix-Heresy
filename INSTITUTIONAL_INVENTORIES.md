# Original institutional inventories

Implementation contract for **3.35b.1b**. [network-inventories.js](network-inventories.js) accounts privately for every retained network, original branch lineage, affiliate/front and covert cell across seven families. It does not establish strategic relevance, intent, service dependence, feasible journeys, political authority or acquired player knowledge. `NetworkCapabilities.assess()` still returns `strategicComplete: false`.

## Original allocation and ownership

The original plan is fixed from canonical source identities, supported function definitions, physical cities/premises, population and commissioning evidence. A new run instantiates its mutable resources before play. The immutable plan retains initial allotments for identity/validation only; its numbers are not a second spendable ledger. Ratings, integrity, player strength, disputes, elapsed play and earlier losses never scale or reroll allocations.

All institutions in a city share one original allocation ceiling: at most one quarter of its original population and one institutional premise per sixteen residents. Existing operating-office people and premises are charged first. Function-specific packages, affiliates and cells then compete in stable source-ID order for that same ceiling. Insufficient support produces an explicit gap, not a silently empty institution or a newly convenient army. This is an original allocation within existing population, not added city population, live hiring, urban construction or a promise of continuous logistics.

Existing office cash, operator, terminal and premises are references to their actual operating account. Network summaries reference separately owned members; consolidated lineages reference their surviving host. Neither creates another reserve, worker or vehicle. Branch charters retain their actual scope. Affiliate/cell ownership and beneficial parentage do not imply a new delegation, principal instruction, credential or political allegiance.

### Fixed authored resource packages

The implementation is authoritative for exact fields; these are deliberately bounded original allotments, not whole-institution wealth estimates inferred from capacity bands.

Ordinary workers/apparatus have bounded work and condition. Existing service-specific allocations preserve their earlier exact duty, fuel, provisions and kit values. Archives contain original local operating/custody notes, not automatic technology unlocks, surveys, government access or player discoveries. New reserve money is not swept into the existing 240-mark office account, used automatically to finance an earlier consumer, or generated as income.

## One mutable owner through materialization

Before an asset appears physically, its mutable state is held in the private inventory. The existing service constructor still establishes layout, qualifications, body/visit requirements and permissible actions. An atomic handoff checks original branch/city/population provenance and transfers the remaining resource fields into that same service's original identities. The inventory then retains only a service reference: its mutable state is cleared, not copied into a second reserve.

Adapters cover passenger transport and the second carrier, mechanics, porters, foreign survey professionals, assay workspaces/actual fixtures, protective professionals, field teams, regulator maintenance, material assessment and commissioned provider communication interfaces. Unsupported or already transferred/lost allocations cannot materialize again. Kits and provisions leave the transferred reserve once when physical stacks are created. Office, municipal, independent supplier and backbone resources retain their existing owners and terms.

Normal updates check original service objects and conflicting aliases without running service constructors or copying balances. Missing owners, changed provenance, substituted identities and conflicting fixtures leave named losses/gaps. A restored-looking object does not heal lost accounting. Actual newly funded export claims remain references to their existing financier/receivable ledger; neither their face value nor an advance is credited again to reserve cash. Wages, rent, deposits, escrow, supplier payments and prior commitments remain solely in their existing service ledgers.

The ordinary reserve receiving-post consumer in [terrestrial-forces.js](terrestrial-forces.js) deliberately keeps its canonical worker, kit and non-office fund in this inventory. The post stores IDs, physical layout, paid-work progress and dated records only. Runtime actor lookup resolves those same objects (including the kit pointer), validating original source/population/owner provenance. Each actual duty minute transfers one mark from that original fund to the same worker; it does not sweep reserve cash into office/deployment funds. Missing initialized posts or assets cannot be reissued. Field-team source eligibility likewise uses the exact original plan allocations, not a historical capacity rating that could omit already allocated people. Existing field/protective/survey commitments remain separate owners/scopes.

## Completeness and migration boundaries

Every source has seven explicit family results: accounted resources, ordinary-function-only support, a supported empty allocation, or unverified accounting. Network/host summaries propagate descendant gaps and deduplicate asset identity. An empty equipment family does not prove an institution harmless or unable to acquire resources later.

Missing source definitions, unsupported premise/function combinations, exhausted original population/premise support, ruined premises, inactive histories and conflicting/missing records remain specific gaps. Dormant/defunct records are retained without inventing healthy staff or fresh reserves, clearing old claims, or declaring all surviving property destroyed. Exceptional launch/orbital/individual means remain unverified until the already-planned exceptional-reach reconciliation supplies actual commissioning/ownership/access facts; an ordinary interface is not a rocket, satellite or spacefarer.

Existing saves enter `legacyUnknown`: already conserved owner resources remain playable and observable, but receive no retroactive money, staff, kit or opposition. Losing an initialized book never requests a new allocation. Continued imports rebind the same resources, independent starts get separate mutable inventories, canonical worlds remain unchanged, and final death freezes updates.

Public capability statements remain the same dated limited accounts. There is no new inventory screen, disclosure ceremony, compulsory contract, live remote telemetry, universal certification or domination flag. Completed .2a/.3a consumers use these same resources; remaining .3b–c work must retain genuinely unresolved facts, and .4 cannot turn accounting coverage alone into strategic completion.
