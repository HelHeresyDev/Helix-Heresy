The twelve five-sided cells are the unavoidable anchors of a seamless hexagonal globe.

Sovereign City Polities

Each fortified city governs itself. Internet diplomacy spans the globe, while physical authority ends near the walls.

Sovereign Authority and Intercity Conflict History

Public records preserve accessions, physically feasible campaigns, tribute, occupation, and revolt. Exact force totals and covert control remain uncertain.

City Charters and Civic Institutions

Each city is its own jurisdiction. This public directory shows chartered authority and institutional capacity; operational weaknesses remain hidden.

Civic Institutional Continuity and Capture History

Public records show overt damage, reform, tribute austerity, occupation direction, and displaced offices. Covert capture and exact operational factors remain hidden.

Current City Law, Enforcement Practice, and Justice Throughput

Every city publishes its own code and declared priorities. Qualitative pipeline conditions show observable capacity, backlog, and delay without inventing cases or conviction rates. Public pressure and temporary directives remain separate from guilt and criminal proof.

Cross-City Recognition and Extradition

Recognition is directional. Choose the city making a request and the city receiving it; foreign warrants provide notice but never execute themselves.

Gods, Faith Networks, and Holy Sites

Every listed identity is objectively real and historically authenticated. Living active gods communicate through finite power and avatars; descent or death can end that contact. Each active god confirms one faith, and local branches remain subject to their independent city.

Divine History and Playable-Year Standing

Public chronicles distinguish silence, descent, and confirmed death. Exact reserves, worship cohorts, concealed survivors, and disputed causes remain hidden.

Succession, Remains, and Lasting Consequences
Religious Institution History

Dated branch records preserve visible founding, damage, displacement, restoration, standing changes, misconduct findings, and authenticated divine censure. Hidden integrity, private attention, concealed misconduct, and covert sponsorship remain private.

Commercial, Research, Military, and Internet Networks

Global communication uses an orbital satellite constellation reinforced by magical relays. Rockets and exceptional individuals can reach space, but network branches, material support, and enforceable authority remain local and capacity-limited.

Non-State Network History

Dated records preserve network founding, service activation, branch damage or relocation, standing changes, institutional conflict, public misconduct findings, and collapse. Covert cells, hidden ownership, exact capacity, integrity, leverage, and private priorities remain private.

Playable-Year City Network Standing
Registered Affiliates and Opaque Shells

Playable-Year Settlements, Routes, and Foundation History

Current population ranges, crowding, services, damage, recovery, and route continuity are resolved from saved history. Exact population, casualties, capacity, readiness, and unresolved displacement remain hidden. Physical connectivity creates no political unity.

Civilization Origins Chronology

Year 0 begins with the first enduring fortified city. Later origin cities began as disconnected projects; public accounts report observed help and surviving evidence rather than private divine motives or exact expenditure.

Civilizational Capabilities and Magitech Eras

Discoveries become capabilities only through resources, power, institutions, defended sites, and maintenance. Knowledge can spread globally while physical deployment remains local and uneven.

City Expansion and Support-Network History

Later sovereign cities retain demographic lineage without inherited allegiance. Corridors and strongholds record practical support, while component bridges join physical networks without creating states or peace.

About Helix Heresy

Helix Heresy is a laboratory-management roguelike about forbidden genetics, physical systems, scientific discovery, and the consequences of ambitious research.

This prototype is developed by Nathaniel Tovar with AI-assisted implementation. It is shared for viewing only; all rights are reserved.
