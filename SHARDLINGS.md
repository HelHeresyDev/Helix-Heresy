# Shardlings

Shardlings are material-specific mineral creatures. **Quartz Shardling** is the
first supported subtype (Research Tier 1, Synthesis Tier 0), not a generic optical
construct. Its natural base forms when a suitable quartz-rich fissure develops a
stable mineral lattice under earth-aspected mana. Laboratory formation manipulates
that process; it does not invent a species or summon a deceased spirit.

## Research and formation

In Research → Natural Creature Formation, read the inherited notes, characterize
quartz, perform the stone and quartz compatibility controls, then document the
pattern. These are inert material controls, not claims of live feeding experiments.
Characterization uses one quartz and one assay reagent; each five-minute control
uses one stone block or quartz, one mineral salt and one drinking water. Documentation
uses one assay reagent. Consumed controls leave physical spent assay material.

Build the shared Elemental Convergence Chamber through ordinary construction;
Mudlet research is not compulsory. A one-hour formation uses one quartz (1 kg),
one mineral salt (0.1 kg), one drinking water (1 L) and two chamber mana. It yields
the original 1 kg body and 1.1 kg spent lattice brine. Physically staged inputs are
reserved, then consumed as incorporated; cancellation cannot refund consumed stock.
Queued procedures replan physical travel if earlier walking or hauling moves the
scientist away; elapsed queue time cannot stand in for attendance.
Service loss spends a finite two-minute failure buffer, not endlessly renewable
free progress. Unused reserved inputs remain recoverable.

Concurrent inherited animancy spends two additional device mana for an own soul.
Unassisted formation yields a soulless body, with no later own-soul retrofit. Both
results retain the original formation record and identity through save/import.

## Diet, care and custody

Compatible minerals, including ordinary stone, sustain it. **Only quartz grows or
replaces its quartz body.** Nominal eating is 10 g/hour, taken from actual adjacent
stock, rubble or contacting rock; quartz assimilation is 25%, with a 2 kg body
limit. Body replacement precedes growth. Remaining consumed mass stays as spent
material until physically collected. Impacts retain real chips, missing body mass
and damage; feeding does not resurrect a dead core. Extreme thermal shock and
mineral starvation can kill.

Offer a physical stone or quartz feed block at the chamber, remove its actual
remainder before offering another, collect retained fragments/waste, and carry or
put down the same original body. Carrying uses normal scientist capacity; the
physical custody item includes the body's retained waste/chip mass and grants no
duplicate creature. A contained or carried body cannot tunnel. No biological sleep
or muscular fatigue is imposed, but finite nourishment, physical access and damage
still matter.

A soul-bearing individual follows its own mineral-seeking behavior. A soulless one
has no initiative, even beside food. The scientist can physically administer one
specific feed block or receive a standing **prospect and feed** instruction through
core contact; cancelling that instruction stops independent-looking activity.
The saved order records its actual contact task. This is a narrow executable
routine, not language, loyalty or a general-purpose worker contract.

## Slow, physical prospecting

Quartz sense reaches six tiles through intervening rock in the current stratum;
it detects actual remaining quartz, not every useful ore. It does not publish a
survey map or reveal unseen hazards. Movement takes five minutes per tile.
Tunnelling applies very slow real structural damage (roughly twelve hours per
ordinary wall before material resistance), with normal rubble, support and hazard
consequences. Walls do not become walkable merely because a target was sensed.
Reinforced/warded barriers and closed doors are not bypassed.

Without a detected growth source it explores and digs locally. On reaching one,
it stays to feed slowly, leaving ample time for the scientist to mine the vein.
Local quartz patches and trace quartz in granite are deterministic; existing
iron/copper/hazard rolls and archived strategic worlds are unchanged. Partial
feeding subtracts from the original cell's excavation budget. Ordinary scientist
mining and rubble hauling recover only the remainder, including fractional lots.
Reloading or importing cannot replenish a deposit or refund eaten material.

The map identifies the same body as **Q** in Canvas and DOM renderers. Examined
body readings are dated observations, not live access to private mineral targets.
No wider beast spawning, additional material subtype, refraction system, strategic
resource survey or follow-up roster is supplied by this package.
