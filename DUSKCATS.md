# Duskcats

Duskcats replace the proposed Shadelet. They are naturally occurring, dark-aspected
biological felines, roughly house-cat sized: not ghosts, condensed shadow or room
dimmers. Their natural habitat includes nocturnal wooded hollows, shaded rocky
cover and cave margins with dark-aspected mana. No strategic population, rarity
rank or new wild encounter is invented by this laboratory package.

## Discovery and development

Research → Natural Creature Formation provides an independent Research Tier 2,
Synthesis Tier 0 development method. No Mudlet, Rootling or elemental chamber is
required. Read inherited notes, characterize sourced feline embryonic tissue,
compare covered and illuminated tissue controls, then document the method.

Travel adds time. Tissue work requires 15–32 °C; the uncovered bright control
requires actual light ≥35. Controls consume their original source lots and retain
spent material and trial receipts, including interrupted work. The covered control
uses its own cloth, not an unexplained dark-room outcome.

Use the original shared synthesis tube, empty and unmoved, with its actual hood
closed and temperature 15–32 °C. Development takes eight hours and four tube mana.
One physically staged arcane feedstock charges eight mana. Concurrent inherited
animancy optionally spends two additional device mana to gather this individual's
own soul. Neither route consumes a donor soul or grants training. Unassisted
development produces a soulless body; no later soul retrofit exists.

The embryo is incorporated first, biomass at one-quarter development, medium at
one-half and water at three-quarters. Interrupted support consumes a nonrenewable
30-minute buffer, then fails; incorporated matter remains consumed and unincorporated
stock is released. No silent soulless substitution occurs. Successful development
retains 1.5 kg spent gestation medium, a 2 kg body, 100 g food and 0.5 L body water
from 4.1 kg inputs. Two of the four tube mana are expended in development; two
remain in the animal. The hood can be reopened for other shared-tube uses; a
closed hood also blocks a later Rootling's required light.

## Actual care, learning and custody

Living bodies metabolize 12 g food and 25 mL water/hour. A quarter of food consumed
can become body growth, capped at 0.5 kg additional mass; the rest remains in the
metabolism ledger. Food, water, growth and mana ledgers persist. There is no
invented corpse consumption, autonomous food gathering or breeding system.
Starvation, dehydration, exhaustion, unsafe temperature and ordinary impacts can
injure and kill. Mana does not heal flesh. Daylight disrupts camouflage, never
burns normal feline flesh. No protective magical cloak bypasses armor or damage.

Administer actual 100 g feline rations (food capacity 600 g). Water care consumes
one real drinking-water unit, administers up to half a litre and leaves the
remainder as actual nonpotable care water. One arcane feedstock supplies eight
mana, maximum sixteen. Ordinary city markets list the sourced embryo and ration;
listing is not a free stock refill or an authored wild population.

Souled animals develop fatigue and voluntarily sleep, stopping pursuit. Handling
requires an awake, sufficiently healthy, fed, watered and unfrightened animal.
Three rewarded familiarity sessions precede separate attack/recall instruction;
each cue requires four actual lessons. Each session consumes 10 g already stored
food, and retains its task/time receipt. No creation-time obedience, speech,
political allegiance or permanent work agreement is implied. A soulless body has
passive physiology but no voluntary sleep, camouflage, hunting, learning or cue
response; assisted feeding and physical transport remain possible.

Make a real reusable carrier with one cloth and one metal part (1.5 kg, 8 L).
Transfer the original animal/remains into that carrier within handling reach,
then carry through ordinary inventory capacity and movement. A living souled cat
requires familiarity and willingness. The occupied carrier includes actual body,
food and water mass, retains its occupant ID, and stays enclosing when dropped.
Opening places that same body on adjacent usable floor and retains the original
empty carrier. Generic elemental release cannot bypass feline handling. An
occupied carrier held by someone else or nested inside another container does
not grant direct examination, care or opening access. Clear the tube only after
the original body is removed. Death retains body, identity,
injuries and creation history; feeding or mana cannot restart the individual.

## Stalking, concealment and pouncing

Below light 35, a loose awake souled cat can blend into existing shade, spending
one mana/hour. It does not alter tile illumination. Ordinary distant vision loses
the body beyond two tiles; direct close observation, contact and supported other
senses are not erased. Both map renderers and handling/target controls respect
current perception; examination retains dated observations rather than revealing
the cat's hidden pursuit target. High ambient mana disruption also breaks blending.

An actual learned local cue demonstrates a currently mutually perceived target,
within six tiles and line of sight, or recalls the cat to the scientist. Cues are
saved with their original target and last-seen location; they do not grant remote
tracking through walls. Goals expire, physical obstacles still block, and movement
is adjacent floor movement, not teleportation. Cat vision works in low light.
Independent predation is bounded to actually perceived small existing loose
slimes; an actual attacker can provoke self-defense. This package spawns no prey.

A pounce visibly prepares for two seconds on the target's observed tile. The
target can move or be lost. Shadow-assisted preparation spends one extra mana
and causes fatigue; bright light or disruption before release strips its bonus
without refund. Recovery takes twelve seconds. Living targets use shared physical
combat accuracy, guard, armor and injuries, not an assassination/instant-death
flag or soul damage. A sourced cloth lure (one real cloth, retained material,
finite condition) offers a safer local demonstration with saved damage receipts.

## Verification boundaries

Focused model checks cover formation, interruption, material/energy accounting,
will, welfare, lessons, perception, movement, pounce dodge/disruption, observations
and final-death freeze. The focused native browser journey earns its own research,
bodies, lessons, carrier and lure, and checks saves/continued import plus both map
renderers. Test adapters relocate original input lots and supply explicit climate,
light-source or impact inputs; they never grant a trained animal or successful
formation. Its long development clock deliberately isolates this feature from
unrelated multi-day city/survival simulation. This is not a claim of a complete
ecosystem, autonomous worker/assassin contract or full-world regression.
