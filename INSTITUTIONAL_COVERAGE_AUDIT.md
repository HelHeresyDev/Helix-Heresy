# Institutional owner-coverage audit

Developer-facing implementation audit of **3.35b.1–.2**, with the resulting finite handoff to **3.35b.3–.4**. Baseline: commit `9a9d43a`, after city-adopted replacement-part standards. This is a static code/source-contract audit, not an inventory of a player's run, a new game mechanic, a public intelligence report or a strategic-completion certificate. No canonical world or run assets were changed for this audit.

## Conclusions

1. Credit the completed operating-account, protection/interdiction, fuel, maintenance/independence, civic communication, receivable-finance and replacement-part-standard consumers. Do not repeat them as new contracts, permissions or compulsory encounters.
2. Reconcile **already implemented branch-backed assets first**. Passenger transport, callout maintenance, field porters, foreign survey escorts and foreign assay workspaces have usable source identities that the current capability reference adapter does not consume.
3. Distinguish those missing links from **genuinely missing inventories**. The retained original network/affiliate/cell records describe history, roles and capacity bands, not complete stocks, capital, staff, equipment or usable access. The small office allocation cannot close that gap.
4. Preserve assets that are independently or municipally owned. A licence, broker introduction, common city, beneficial owner, shared provider or historical relationship is not title or a command charter.
5. The existing conservative result is deliberate: `NetworkCapabilities.assess()` returns `strategicComplete: false`, and `NetworkOperations.fact()` can report a supported local challenge but otherwise remains unverified. Do not remove these guards merely because more references have been connected.
6. Finish through the eight named packages below, inside the existing four deliverables. This audit is complete; another general audit, disclosure ceremony or one-service-per-institution ladder is not a prerequisite.
