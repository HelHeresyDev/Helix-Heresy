# Hauntbound: inhabited objects, willing workers and residual enchantment

Hauntbound are not a producible family. A deceased individual's lingering soul
inhabits an object to which they were strongly attached during life instead of
entering reincarnation. A newly gathered soul has no such lived attachment.
There is no recipe, production tier, artificial soulless counterpart or inherited
creation-device shortcut. Everything creatable exists naturally; not everything
natural must be creatable. Artificial-formation acceptance rules do not apply.

The inhabitant remains the same soul, with diminished intelligence, fragmented
memories, personality and an unresolved attachment. They are not automatically
obedient, loyal, fluent in ordinary speech or equipped with their former expertise.
Direct spirit conversation requires the scientist's personal Animancy [Initiate],
no magical suppression, actual local attendance and one personal mana per exchange.
The inherited creation device is not a spirit telephone. Ordinary people can
observe movement or infer meaning, but cannot normally converse directly. This
provides discretion, not invisibility, guaranteed silence or immunity from witnesses.

## First acquisition and relationship

Research contains **Hauntbound — inhabited objects**. One original cloth keepsake
and accompanying old farewell are inherited among the laboratory's effects. They
are initialized once in the mutable run, never added to its archived world. An
independent run has its own episode; a continued import retains the original body,
spirit, letter and history. Missing or destroyed originals are not regenerated.

Examine the actual keepsake, then establish animantic contact. Mara remembers a
departure and a promise to keep waiting, not a complete autobiography. An actual
folded farewell provides independent evidence that those who left reached shelter
and released her from that promise. Read the original letter, then personally
share it. This makes a voluntary goodbye possible but **does not dismiss Mara**.
There is no invented living recipient or automatic reincarnation reward.

Mara can instead agree to continuing safe light-materials work. Her terms preserve
the letter and exclude harmful or hazardous assignments. A badly damaged body or
soul prevents a new agreement. Directly sensed dangerous conditions or loss of the
letter can withdraw consent; repairing an object does not buy its inhabitant.
Reaching acceptance leaves the worker route available until a separate goodbye.

## Useful continuing work and soul force

An agreed assignment enters the existing task and work-order queue with Mara as
its worker, not the scientist. Select a personally visible original safe material
lot, a whole quantity and a distinct visible floor destination. Mara independently
travels, picks up real cargo, delivers it and returns for further trips until that
assignment is fulfilled. Further assignments are repeatable. Locked/closed doors,
walls, access, occupancy and the original source's position remain authoritative.
No arbitrary remote orders, teleportation, free stock, specimen handling or hidden
laboratory knowledge are supplied. Cancellation requires personal communication
and drops carried cargo at the worker's actual location. Interrupted/blocked work,
source reservations and original carried stack IDs survive ordinary saves/imports.
Work reports are locally received and stay dated while away.
The inhabited body and its original letter are not automatically hauled as bulk
stock. Actual cargo must be put down before its inhabited carrier can itself be
carried; an object's small nominal mass cannot conceal another actor's load.

This doll weighs 0.5 kg and has 20 N maximum soul force, up to 1 kg / 3 L cargo
capacity. Compatible current material lots are cloth, metal parts, rubber and
other explicitly listed small inert supplies; source units must actually fit.
Whole glass stock is too heavy for this doll despite being an inert material.
Body, absorbed water and cargo weight all count against force. Motion consumes
finite soul exertion; an exhausted spirit independently pauses and recovers before
continuing. It requires no biological meals, drinking water, sleep or muscle rest.
Performed work earns its own saved experience, not the scientist's skills.

General worldbuilding also permits inhabited bicycles, tools and mechs. A spirit's
limited force can move a doll or pedal a bicycle, but cannot move an unpowered mech.
Working powered machinery supplies its own strength through supported controls;
possession grants no fuel, repair, software access, sensors or expert piloting.
This package implements the doll, not a bicycle/mech or general possession engine.

## Body damage and care

Cloth seams wear through actual work. Severe wear, waterlogging or bodily damage
blocks work. Supported contacting water spills wet the cloth by transferring real
water into its body mass; ordinary drying takes time and retains an evaporation
ledger. This is not a general chemical-soaking simulator.
Direct extreme heat burns the body. Physical attacks use ordinary combat damage;
soul attacks can harm the original soul separately. Breaking the doll retains its
bound soul in the remains; it does not force reincarnation or destroy the soul.

A three-minute attended repair consumes one original nearby cloth, restores up to
35 body condition and reduces seam wear by 40. Replaced fabric remains as 0.5 kg
of physical worn-cloth waste; new cloth replaces old material, not a new person.
Repair neither erases injuries/history nor heals or recreates a destroyed soul.
Body repair can restore usable bound remains, but work needs renewed consent.

## Optional goodbye and enchanted-item route

After hearing the original farewell, Mara can choose reincarnation through a
separate attended goodbye. Active work must first finish or be canceled with cargo
physically put down. The inhabitant departs; no trapped or consumed soul powers the
item afterward, and no worker remains available.

Inhabiting the object imprints one charge per hour, up to 20. The same original
doll, including damage and repairs, retains that accumulated protective enchantment.
Physically carry the released keepsake to cushion physical-only damage by 30%, at
most three points per hit and one charge per absorbed point. Mixed, fire, soul and
other damage bypass it. Progressive injuries are not retrospectively prevented;
there is no healing, invulnerability or remote inventory bonus. A broken keepsake
provides no protection.

After departure, one physically staged arcane feedstock recharges eight points if
all eight fit. It consumes the original lot and retains its source receipt. Waiting
no longer replenishes the item, and charging never recalls the departed person.
Canvas and DOM maps identify the observed object/inhabitant without exposing an
unexamined spirit's history. Final scientist death freezes this system normally.
