# Creature formation: Mudlets and Cinderlings

Every supported family has a naturally existing base form. The scientist modifies
its formation process; neither science nor gods invent life from nothing. An
engineered variant need not occur naturally. Research Tier describes discovery
difficulty; Synthesis Tier describes practical production complexity, not power.

## Apparatus and research

The starting laboratory contains the original creation-time animancy device with
24 mana of fuel. It gathers soul energy while artificial bodies form. It neither
stores souls nor performs resurrection. One physically staged arcane feedstock
charges eight mana, up to 24. Duplicate device construction is not unlocked.

Use Research → Natural Elemental Formation. Personally read the inherited notes,
then characterize one common clay and one drinking water at the workbench. That
allows a 2×1 Elemental Convergence Chamber: four metal parts, two glass and two
arcane feedstock, built through ordinary construction. Charge it, perform the
five-minute stabilization trial (one clay, one water, one chamber mana), then
document the observed pattern at the workbench using one assay reagent.

Stage the named original physical input lots within one tile of the apparatus's
operator position. Tasks reserve those lots and require actual travel, attendance
and usable equipment; a resource total or UI click alone produces no evidence.
Characterization/stabilization leave physical clay-water residue, including when
interrupted after incorporating their inputs. Other unused reserved supplies are
released on cancellation. The normal physical stock controls handle staging;
test-only stock relocation is not a gameplay reward.

## Forming and caring for a Mudlet

Mudlets naturally arise in wet clay under sustained earth/water mana convergence.
The documented chamber method reproduces that pattern using two clay and two
water, two chamber mana and ten minutes of stabilization. Research and Synthesis
Tier are both zero. Its saved pattern is a bounded procedure, not a genome or an
unrestricted creature designer. Full natural-beast encounters remain deferred.

Choose the animancy connection **before** loading an attempt. A connected attempt
requires the same inherited device, in the same room within eight tiles and line
of sight, and two additional mana supplied over formation. Disabling, exhausting,
destroying or moving required apparatus pauses growth; a nonrenewable two-minute
buffer eventually fails the attempt. Unused original lots and incorporated
residue remain. A requested soul-bearing attempt never silently becomes soulless.

The unassisted procedure instead produces a soulless Mudlet. Completed provenance
cannot be changed by reconnecting the device. The creature has one retained body,
two kilograms of clay and initially two litres of water. Evaporation depends on
actual temperature/humidity, changes consistency, and eventually causes injury
and death. Wetting adds real water; surplus becomes a physical runoff lot.
Dryness is reversible before fatal injury, not a free health reset.

The scientist can physically release the body/remains to adjacent floor or position
a living body at its original nearby chamber's test fitting. No spoken commands,
worker role, communication or long-distance transport is implied. A thirsty souled
loose Mudlet may move toward directly sensed adjacent exposed runoff. A soulless
one chooses no action; passive water absorption and physical damage still occur.

The fitting holds four litres in its reservoir and eight in its catch basin.
A sufficiently moist living body reduces low-pressure leakage by 90%; drying or
high pressure reopens flow, and high pressure erodes the body. Water is conserved
between reservoir and basin. Draining deposits the actual water on the floor;
there is no infinite water supply or remote plumbing repair. Examine locally for
current readings; off-site body, run, fuel and fitting observations stay dated.

## Forming and caring for a Cinderling

Cinderlings naturally form around charred burn sites or volcanic vents where
combustible material sustains flame-aspected mana. A coherent ember core and flame
are the natural base body, not a new species invented by the scientist. Research
and Synthesis Tier are both zero; wild encounters remain outside this package.

After reading the inherited notes, characterize one charcoal at the workbench.
Either charcoal or clay/water characterization unlocks the **same** convergence
chamber: Mudlet discovery is not compulsory. Perform a five-minute contained
ignition/stability trial using one charcoal and one chamber mana, then document
the observed flame pattern with one assay reagent. Consumed trials leave physical
assay residue or ash and smoke, including interrupted trials; they are not free
research clicks or refunded materials.

Load **two charcoal**, then sustain **two chamber mana over ten minutes**. There
is no biomass input. The shared optional animancy connection requires two more
mana from the actual inherited device during this same formation window. The
original lots, partial incorporation, two-minute outage buffer and sealed soul
outcome follow the Mudlet contract. Failure leaves incorporated residue and
releases only unused inputs; it creates neither a body nor a replacement soul.

A living Cinderling begins with two finite fuel units, holds at most four, and
normally burns one per hour (one quarter per hour when cold and smoldering).
Administering one actual charcoal adds one unit. Consumed material remains in the
saved ledger as ash (10%) and emissions (90%). Actual local warmth and light
depend on burning fuel; closed barriers and normal light propagation still apply.
Ventilation failure stops combustion, while starvation, suffocation, cold and
unsafe contamination can injure and kill. Dead hot remains lose only residual
heat, do not burn more fuel, and cannot be fed or rekindled into the same person.

Hot feeding, release and transfer require the original usable **long tongs** in
physical reach or carried by the scientist. Handling reserves and wears that
same durable tool, not an abstract inventory total. A nearby empty **fire-resistant
brazier** costs two stone blocks and two metal parts, with five minutes of ordinary
construction. It holds one original body and protects against loose contact
ignition; it is not a second convergence machine, controller or fuel source.
The intact chamber also contains contact ignition, with reduced outward light/heat.

A loose burning body accumulates contact ignition against actual exposed charcoal,
lumber or cloth on its own tile. Sustained contact consumes original stock and
leaves physical ash and smoke. Reserved/container-held lots are excluded. This is
a bounded local hazard, not a global wildfire or firefighting simulation. Direct
scientist contact can burn. Water quenching consumes real water, leaves retained
water/steam, damages the ember core and can permanently kill it; contact with an
actual Mudlet runoff pool also quenches. It is not reversible desiccation care.

A hungry souled loose Cinderling may move to directly sensed adjacent exposed
charcoal and ingest the original unit. Neither the brazier nor soul presence
implies language, obedience or a work role. Soulless bodies never choose movement
or self-feeding, even beside fuel; passive combustion, cooling and ignition still
occur. Both outcomes retain identity, soul provenance, injuries, fuel, ash and
observations through saves and continued imports. Local examinations are dated;
both Canvas and DOM maps use a `C`/remains glyph fallback, not claimed new bitmap art.

## Shared soul rules and compatibility

New artificial slime synthesis, controlled reproduction and homunculus growth use
the same creation-window distinction. Ordinary natural births and already living
individuals are not rewritten. An artificial brood requires its own gathered soul
energy for each child; parents retain their identities. Homunculi keep their
advanced research, skills, tissue-template, care and utility requirements.
Late-game soul-beacon receiving bodies do not acquire a new independent soul.

Soulless creatures cannot level, develop voluntary work/learning, seek food, test
containment or initiate combat/retreat. This does not disable passive metabolism,
digestion or damage, nor prevent scientist-administered care. Construct orders
need a family-specific supported control channel; none is invented here. Slimes
cannot communicate or cooperate, with or without a soul. A soul guarantees neither
human intellect nor obedience, language, loyalty or consent.

Saved original identities, sealed soul records, stock custody, work progress,
finite interruption buffers, moisture, injuries and observations survive continued
runs. Existing individuals without new provenance retain their established soul,
skills and history rather than being retroactively made soulless. New Mudlets use
an `M`/remains identification cue in both map renderers, plus native Research
inspection; production bitmap art is not claimed for this new family.
