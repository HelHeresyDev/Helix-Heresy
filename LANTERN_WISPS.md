# Lantern Wisps

Lantern Wisps are naturally existing luminous arcane creatures: a fragile mineral
core develops a coherent mantle where luminous minerals accumulate under sustained
light-aspected mana, such as caves and shaded rocky hollows. They are not deceased
spirits, invented life, or miniature human workers. This package authors their
natural family/habitat without adding wild encounters, strategic populations or a
rarity rank to archived worlds.

## Research and formation

Research Tier 2 describes discovering the method; Synthesis Tier 0 describes its
comparatively simple repeatable production. No Mudlet research is compulsory.

| Procedure | Actual inputs | Attended time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Characterize | 1 glow salts + 1 assay reagent | 120 s |
| Fit reusable focusing insert | 1 glass | 60 s |
| Stable mineral control | 1 glow salts + 1 chamber mana | 300 s |
| Overcharged control | 1 glow salts + 2 chamber mana | 300 s |
| Document comparison | 1 assay reagent | 180 s |
| Load formation | 1 glow salts (100 g) | 60 s |

Read inherited notes first. Characterization unlocks the existing shared convergence
chamber, whose ordinary construction costs remain separate. The glass insert is an
actual attached item, with original material/source IDs, not a free prerequisite
flag. Controls retain their spent original material and paid mana/coherence
receipts, even when interrupted. They are inert sample studies, not secretly
created soulless individuals.

One cleared, enabled chamber with its original focusing insert sustains formation
for ten minutes, spending four chamber mana: two expended and two retained in the
new body. Salts incorporate at initiation and become the 100 g core. Optional
concurrent inherited animancy spends two additional device mana to gather this
individual's own soul; no donor identity, initial training or later soul retrofit
exists. Without participation the result is soulless. The original apparatus must
stay in place; missing support consumes a nonrenewable two-minute buffer and then
fails, retaining incorporated salts as inactive physical material. Requested
soul-bearing formation never silently substitutes a soulless creature.

## Light, care and fragility

Emission uses actual world lighting, propagation and barriers, and can expose the
scientist as readily as illuminate a workspace. Bright emission has intensity 60,
radius five tiles and costs two mana/hour. At reserves of two or less, a souled
wisp voluntarily dims to intensity 20, radius two, costing half a mana/hour. A
soulless body does not make this self-preservation choice. Zero reserve emits no
light; prolonged depletion costs ten coherence-health/hour and eventually kills.
Disruptive ambient mana and extreme temperature also cause real damage.

One actual feedstock supplies eight mana, capacity sixteen; original source
receipts persist. No food, water or biological sleep is required. Recharging never
heals an injured core or restarts a dead individual. Physical core hits inflict
three times their incoming damage in health; fractures retain original mineral
mass. Inactive core/fragments, injuries and formation identity remain after death.

## Learned following and ordinary physical space

Three rewarded familiarity sessions precede three lessons for each of follow and
stay. Each session takes one minute and spends 0.05 actual stored mana. The
soul-bearing animal must be sufficiently fueled, healthy, unfrightened and able
to perceive the local demonstration. An opaque shutter must be opened for
training. Familiarity is not speech, political allegiance or guaranteed obedience.

Each learned signal takes ten seconds and lasts at most five minutes. Following
uses the scientist's actually visible location within six tiles; ordinary light,
line of sight and barriers still apply. The wisp remembers only the last seen
location and loses the signal after fifteen seconds without perception. It does
not track the scientist through walls or across strata. Stay holds its current
position; expiry is not an invented replacement task. Injury, depletion,
disruption and enclosure can interrupt cooperation.

Low hovering moves one adjacent tile per two seconds using ordinary traversable
same-stratum floor paths. Walls, closed doors and sealed glass block the body;
there is no door operation, phasing, shaft traversal, high-altitude flight,
remote survey or cargo-carrying role. Soulless bodies have passive emission and
damage but no learning, following, wandering or independent fuel seeking.

## Physical lantern housing

One glass and one metal part, with two minutes of attended work, produce a reusable
transparent housing with an opaque shutter. Transfer the original core into an
actual staged empty housing; a living souled animal needs familiarity and
willingness. Take/set down use ordinary inventory capacity, custody and movement.
The occupied item's mass includes the original core/fragments. Dropping leaves
the body enclosed; opening releases that same body onto adjacent usable floor,
retaining the original empty housing. Other holders and enclosing containers do
not grant direct care or access.

The shutter hides outgoing light but does not suppress internal mana expenditure.
It cannot extend life for free. Housing light comes from its actual physical
location, including after carrying or setting it down. Opaque enclosing storage
blocks the outward source; merely changing who carries a lantern does not.

The original housing has finite condition: it absorbs up to 25 cumulative physical
damage, losing four condition per absorbed point. Once shattered, it releases the
same core at the housing's location; excess impact reaches the fragile core.
Original broken glass/metal remain, without a replacement creature or free repair.
Nonphysical attacks do not gain this physical shell protection.

## Validation boundaries

Focused models cover formation, interruption, will, learning, finite mana/light,
following/stay, barriers/lost perception, fractures, housing protection, saved
identity and final scientist-death freeze. The native Chromium journey earns its
research, chamber, focusing insert, both soul outcomes, lessons and housing from
original supplies. It checks real following/light, shutters, retained broken
housing, failed requested formation, Canvas/DOM, saves and continued import.
Test adapters only stage original stocks or apply explicit light-source, movement
and impact inputs, never grant successful research or a trained individual.
The isolated feature clock is not a claim of full-world survival/regression.
